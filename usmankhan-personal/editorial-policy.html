Personal views and independence

This is my personal website. The opinions, interpretations and conclusions in its blogs, project journal and other original commentary are my own. They do not represent the views, policies or official positions of any current or former employer, client, partner or other organisation with which I am associated.

I write in my personal capacity and am not speaking on behalf of those organisations. A reference to my employment, a professional engagement, a company or a product does not by itself imply that an organisation has reviewed, approved, sponsored or endorsed the content.

Accuracy, interpretation and changing information

I aim to present information accurately and distinguish my interpretation from the sources I reference. I can make mistakes. Content may contain errors or omissions, reflect incomplete information, or become outdated as products, practices and circumstances change. My views may also change as I learn more.

Publication dates provide context; an article is not a promise that every statement remains current. Where I discuss another organisation’s products or approach, consult its current official documentation for its own position. Where an article relies on a source, read my commentary alongside that source and its context.

Sources, examples and third-party material

Original commentary is presented as my perspective; quotations, linked material and attributed statements remain those of their respective sources. References and links provide context and do not imply endorsement in either direction. Third-party names, trade marks, images and other materials remain subject to their owners’ rights.

Hypothetical examples illustrate an idea and should not be read as accounts of an actual company or incident. Descriptions of professional work reflect the stated scope of my contribution. Past outcomes are specific to their context and are not guarantees of future results.

Using this content

The content is provided for general information and discussion. It is not a professional assessment of your circumstances or a substitute for advice appropriate to your situation. Independently verify material information and test technical suggestions in a suitable environment before relying on them for a decision or implementation.

I do not guarantee that the content is complete, current, error-free or suitable for a particular purpose. Nothing in this policy excludes or restricts liability that cannot lawfully be excluded, or changes any rights you have under applicable law.

Corrections and concerns

If you believe I have made a factual error, misrepresented a position, omitted important context or used material inappropriately, please email usman@usman-khan.com. Include the page address, the passage concerned and any supporting source or explanation.

I will review credible concerns and correct, clarify or remove material where appropriate. For substantive corrections, I aim to note the change on the affected article so readers can understand what was updated.
