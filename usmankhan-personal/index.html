THE THREAD THROUGH EVERY STAGE

An objective
maturity model.

I use agreed criteria and observable evidence to assess capabilities across people, process, technology and governance. Together, we establish the baseline, set the target maturity and reassess progress against the same criteria.

Each rating has a defined meaning, supporting evidence and a clear next step.

Describe what each maturity level looks like in practice and agree the capabilities that matter to your goals.
02 / Assess the evidence
Use delivery data, documented practices and stakeholder input. Make gaps in evidence explicit.
03 / Demonstrate progress
Reassess at roadmap milestones, compare against the baseline and connect capability improvements to business outcomes.
